## 5. GENERAL RULES captured (High unless noted)
- App is mandatory. You scan QR codes on the card backs, holding them about 15 cm above the screen. Hero and class cards are scanned at seating [16:27–17:48].
- LP and markers are tracked analogue. The app doesn't track hero LP [26:40, 36:13].
- Round: (1) the boss prepares attacks and shows the planned damage per hero, plus effects and shields per type (Nah/Fern/Magie shown at the bottom). (2) Heroes act: the team chooses who starts, then players alternate one card each. Each hero gets 3 actions (3 pips on the icon) [22:35, 23:16]. (3) The boss attacks. (4) Poison/effects resolve. (5) Discard any number of hand cards. (6) Refill to hand size. The help card numbers the action phase as Phase 3 and discard as Phase 6 [82:03, 117:30]. Bosses can break this order (interrupts, reactions) [30:45].
- Card anatomy: the top-left symbol is the damage type (melee, ranged, magic). The number is damage.
  - "+" = enhancer. It can only be played once an attack of that type was played this round by anyone [20:31, 24:38, 55:21]. In practice it adds to the running type total.
  - Lightning (Blitz) = play another card afterwards (extra action) [28:42, 41:00].
  - Star = special effect, read the text [41:00].
  - Hourglass = value equals the round number [41:41].
  - Shield/armour cards protect yourself or another hero [21:13]. Some cards give shields to two heroes [33:29].
- Shields: boss shields per type must be beaten before damage goes through. Damage below the shield does nothing; damage above it passes [30:04]. Focus one type to break through [95:02].
- Poison: each marker = extra damage each round (1 per marker baseline; 2 on the Prince). You only receive the attack's poison if you take damage from it. A fully blocked attack gives no poison [29:24–30:04]. Poison resolves after the boss attack [33:29]. Markers stay until removed.
- After the boss attack: discard any number of cards, then draw back up to hand size. When the deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile [28:02–28:42].
- Some cards remove themselves from the game for the rest of the fight [60:08] (e.g. Diadem via Déjàvu, Antiboss 3000).
- Heroes at ≤0 LP = defeat. Optionally keep playing with kids [27:20].
- Healing potion: 8 LP, once per hero per fight, costs an action. Rucksack: draw 3, as an action, reusable. Glücksbringer: play the top card of the draw pile, lightning ignored, reusable. Medizintasche: remove 2 markers, reusable.
- Advanced deck building ("Fortgeschrittene Kampfvorbereitung", rulebook, from Boss 2) [142:39–145:21] (High):
  - Deck must have at least 20 cards.
  - At most 1 loot card per star level in the deck.
  - Before each boss fight, for each extra (loot) card you have added, you may leave one card out.
- Boss abilities have symbols: reaction (jaw/teeth), recurring (hourglass), etc. Antiboss 3000 (Dwarf card, single use) prevents the next boss ability [59:28, 63:33] (Medium on scope). "Fluch des Raben" (Mage/Rogue card): 2 instant damage, then lay it next to the hero and discard it to cancel one boss ability of the matching symbol [68:22, 123:38].
- Cards mentioned (names): Wurfmesser, Gajas Bogen (4 ranged + a hero of choice draws 1), Schlagring 3, Wampenschoner (4 shield to any hero), Sonnenfeuer (magic), Sternenklinge 3, Morgenstern (5 melee, 2 self-damage), Energiesog (magic + 2 LP to a hero), Titanarmschienen (2 shield to two heroes), Lichtschild 3, Schattenrobe, Felsspalter 4, Feuerball, magische Explosion (magic +), Stollensprenger 2, Diadem der Unsichtbarkeit (next attack +3, extra action; not on enhancers), Hornissenstachel 2 (+ extra action), Vieperzahn (2 + poison on boss), Rune des Königs (all draw 1, extra action), Rauchbombe (2 shield, remove status, extra action), Déjàvu (play from discard or top of deck; that card is exiled), Gratisbogen, Helm der Brettspielgarde (prevent 2, remove 1 poison/status, extra action), Splitter, Meteoritenschauer (random damage on all three types, self LP loss 2), Dämonischer Pakt (5 shield, lose 2 LP), Runenstein (damage = round), magischer Pfeilsturm 3, Fluch des Raben, Überladung, Giftklinge (poisons the boss), Ritualdolch (charges up), Dornenfalle 4, Lyrias Ring (all heroes +2 LP, extra action), Handarmbrust, Pfeilsalve, gezielter Schuss (4/5), Schutzzauber, Flammenschild (magic enh 2 + shield 2), Klinge des Windes, Wurfschild des Gladiators, Monokel, Goldene Mitte, Schattenklinge, Wurzahn/Werzahn.
